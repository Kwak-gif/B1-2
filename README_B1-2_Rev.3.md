## 4. 통합 영상·오디오 프롬프트

**영상 프롬프트 (오픈AI, shuka_final.mp4)**
Create a single 8-second vertical 9:16 animated shortform ad in flat vector
illustration style. Keep the same cartoon art style and consistent characters.
[0-3s] A cartoon young man overwhelmed, holding his head, surrounded by floating
charts, stock numbers and question marks in a dark moody newsroom. Slow zoom-in.
[3-6s] Bright flash transition to a cheerful presenter smiling, pointing at rising
golden arrows, coins and cash icons in a bright studio. Smooth pan-right.
[6-8s] Zoom out to reveal channel logo at top and red subscribe button at bottom.
Thumbs up, confetti, logo bounce. Hold final frame.
Consistent flat vector style, smooth transitions, 9:16 vertical, celebratory mood.

**내레이션 (오픈AI TTS, 8초)**
> "Feeling lost in the market? You're not alone. Join us for clear, friendly breakdowns that turn confusion into confidence. Subscribe and grow!"
